How do I start my exam? ▼
Log in with your Candidate ID and Access Code from the portal, verify your camera and audio, read instructions, and click Start Exam.
What are the system requirements? ▼
Windows 10/11 or macOS, 4GB RAM minimum, active webcam and microphone, stable 2Mbps+ broadband connection.
What should I do if my exam is not loading? ▼
Check your internet connection, verify that the local exam daemon is running on port 5000, and ensure all unauthorized background apps are terminated.
Can I switch tabs during the exam? ▼
No. Tab switching, Alt+Tab, and external windows trigger immediate security violation strikes. Reaching 10 strikes will terminate your exam.
What happens if my internet connection is lost? ▼
ExamFort saves your answers locally in real-time. Once the connection is restored, answers are automatically synchronized with the server.
How will I get my exam results? ▼
Automated multiple-choice and coding results appear on your completed screen immediately upon submission.
